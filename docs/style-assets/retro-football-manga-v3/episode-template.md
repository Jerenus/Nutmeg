# Retro Football Manga v3 Episode Template

## Standard 60-Second Episode Structure

Each match becomes one 60-second vertical episode split into six 10-second scenes.

### Segment 01: Opening Title / Tunnel Standoff

Purpose: introduce the two fictional teams and the match atmosphere.

Required visuals:

- manga page flips open or old TV title card appears;
- both teams line up in a tunnel, center circle, or rain-lit stadium entrance;
- two core players exchange a determined look;
- title card names the fixture in fictional team language;
- no real crest, no official kit sponsor.

Narration role:

- one-sentence hook;
- explain why the match is about a tactical conflict, not only stronger vs weaker.

### Segment 02: Home / Primary Team Weapon

Purpose: show the main strength of the first team.

Possible variables:

- high press;
- wing overload;
- central tempo control;
- star forward isolation;
- rest-defense stability;
- home atmosphere.

Required visuals:

- close-up of boots, eyes, captain armband, or ball control;
- hand-drawn motion lines;
- teammates forming a tactical shape;
- one large keyword card, such as `PRESS`, `TEMPO`, `WIDTH`, or Chinese equivalent.

### Segment 03: Away / Opponent Counterweapon

Purpose: show how the opponent can break the first team's plan.

Possible variables:

- counterattack lane;
- target striker hold-up play;
- set-piece threat;
- midfield press resistance;
- fullback channel attack;
- goalkeeper distribution.

Required visuals:

- opponent's key player activates a contrasting move;
- field splits into manga panels;
- speed line or impact burst crosses the screen;
- do not show a definitive goal unless it is framed as a simulated highlight.

### Segment 04: Tactical Board / Data Variables

Purpose: convert analysis into a visual tactical explainer.

Required visuals:

- hand-drawn tactics board or manga notebook page;
- arrows, zones, and pressure circles;
- 2-3 keywords only;
- optional retro UI frame, but as analysis overlay, not gambling UI.

Suggested keywords:

- `开局压迫`
- `反击通道`
- `第一粒进球`
- `中场节拍`
- `定位球`
- `体能`

### Segment 05: Simulated Highlight / Climax

Purpose: give the viewer a memorable football-action scene.

Required visuals:

- one simulated chance, tackle, shot, save, or transition race;
- dramatic manga cut-in of eyes and ball spin;
- exaggerated but plausible sports action;
- no final score claim.

This scene should be built from the tactical read. If the analysis says the opponent threatens in transition, the highlight should be a transition race, not a random bicycle kick.

### Segment 06: Retro Scoreboard / Closing Suspense

Purpose: end with a clear but compliant take-away.

Required visuals:

- old analog scoreboard or manga chapter-end card;
- no final result displayed as fact;
- show the key viewing question instead, e.g. `Can the press survive the first wave?`;
- closing responsible-viewing subtitle.

Narration role:

- summarize the core variable;
- include the disclaimer: `以上只是赛前数据观察，不构成任何投注建议，理性看球。`

## Focus Match Variant

For focus matches, keep six 10-second scenes.

## Standard Match Variant

For lower-priority matches, compress to four 15-second scenes:

1. Opening + team weapon
2. opponent counterweapon
3. tactical board + simulated highlight
4. retro scoreboard + closing suspense
