# Retro Football Manga v3.1 Style Bible

## Purpose

Retro Football Manga v3.1 turns Nutmeg match analysis into a repeatable football animation column. The style is not tied to a league, club, player, manga, game, studio, or artist. Daily teams and tactical variables are injected into this fixed system.

## Core Creative Direction

Original 1980s retro youth-football manga animation with short-video pacing.

The output should feel like a lost hand-drawn football comic adapted into a weekly animated analysis column:

- refined hand-inked linework, not rough sketch;
- clean cel-shaded color, not glossy 3D or photoreal broadcast;
- aged manga page texture, subtle analog warmth, restrained film jitter;
- sincere youth-sports emotion and tactical clarity;
- recurring series modules: opening hook card, manga cover, weapon card, counterweapon card, tactical notebook, simulated replay, retro scoreboard, closing suspense card;
- vertical-first composition for mobile viewing.

## Production Factors

Every generated segment must inherit these factors:

1. 前2秒强钩子：first frame must show a readable suspense question, large face, ball, scoreboard, or comic-cover clash.
2. 竖屏可读：9:16 scenes use large subjects and only 1-3 readable Chinese keywords.
3. 固定栏目包装：opening, tactical notebook, simulated replay, scoreboard, and closing card recur every day.
4. 角色连续性：within one match episode, fictional team colors, numbers, hairstyles, body shapes, and roles remain stable.
5. 精修赛璐璐动画：clean linework, stable anatomy, and finished cel color; never messy AI sketch.
6. 平台分发：Douyin emphasizes hook and completion; Xiaohongshu emphasizes cover, title, and card-save value.
7. 合规边界：no real club marks, player likenesses, named IP imitation, gambling visuals, or final-result certainty.

## Visual Anchors

- Expressive 1980s sports-manga faces with angular brows and determined eyes.
- Fine black ink detail lines with thicker outer contour lines.
- Cel-shaded team-color blocks with limited but memorable palettes.
- Manga motion grammar: speed lines, concentration lines, impact bursts, panel gutters, sweat drops, dust trails.
- Football-specific shots: boots on grass, ball spin, pressing arrows, sideline board, tactical notebook, low-angle shot posture.
- Analog texture: paper grain, halftone dots, subtle flicker, warm old-TV title-card color.

## Forbidden Direction

Avoid:

- direct imitation of named manga, anime, game, studio, artist, or character IP;
- real club crests, exact kit patterns, official sponsors, or real player likenesses;
- photoreal football broadcast footage;
- glossy 3D, mobile-game gacha art, esports poster lighting, plastic skin;
- rough unfinished sketch, low-detail childish cartoon, generic AI anime face;
- betting slips, odds terminals, money, casino imagery, or gambling actions;
- displaying a final score or result as certainty before the match.

## Team Abstraction Rule

Real teams become fictional youth-football teams. Borrow only broad city energy, color inspiration, and tactical identity.

Example pattern:

- Paris blue/red side -> `Paris Star XI`, abstract star-and-tower symbol, no real crest.
- Munich red/white side -> `Munich Red Thunder`, abstract lightning-and-mountain symbol, no real crest.

## Match Analysis Boundary

Public videos explain football variables: pressure, transition, rest defense, first goal, set pieces, lineup risk, tempo, and tactical matchups.

Internal notes may include market observations, but public scripts must not sound like betting recommendations. Use the disclaimer: `以上只是赛前数据观察，不构成任何投注建议，理性看球。`
