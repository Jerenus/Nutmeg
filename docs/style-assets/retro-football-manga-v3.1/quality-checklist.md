# Retro Football Manga v3.1 Quality Checklist

Use this before approving Segment 01 or spending tokens on remaining segments.

## Scroll Stop

- [ ] First frame works as a thumbnail.
- [ ] The first 2 seconds contain a clear hook question or conflict.
- [ ] Main face/ball/action is clear on mobile; all readable text comes from local postproduction, not Seedance.

## Style Fit

- [ ] Looks like original 1980s youth-football manga animation.
- [ ] Shows refined hand-inked linework and cel shading.
- [ ] Uses paper grain, speed lines, concentration lines, or panel gutters.
- [ ] Does not look like rough AI sketch, 3D render, broadcast footage, or esports poster.

## Continuity

- [ ] Fictional team colors are consistent.
- [ ] Character numbers, hairstyles, and roles are stable.
- [ ] Scene feels connected to the same episode.
- [ ] Fixed packaging modules are recognizable.

## Football Relevance

- [ ] Visualizes a real football variable from the analysis.
- [ ] Simulated highlight matches the tactical read.
- [ ] Seedance raw video contains no readable text, letters, numbers, fake subtitles, or garbled characters.
- [ ] Local postproduction tactical text uses no more than 1-3 keywords per screen.
- [ ] Ending preserves uncertainty instead of claiming a result.

## Compliance

- [ ] No real club crest or sponsor.
- [ ] No real player likeness.
- [ ] No named manga, anime, game, studio, artist, or character IP.
- [ ] No betting slip, odds board, money, casino, or gambling action.
- [ ] Public script includes: `以上只是赛前数据观察，不构成任何投注建议，理性看球。`

## Postproduction

- [ ] Chinese voiceover is generated as one continuous master track, not isolated 10-second slots.
- [ ] The final upload master has no baked background music; choose BGM from the publishing platform.
- [ ] Series header, tactical cards, subtitles, and responsible-use line are rendered locally with consistent fonts and layout.

## Approval Rule

If any of scroll stop, style fit, continuity, compliance, or football relevance fails, do not generate the remaining segments yet. Regenerate Segment 01 or revise the team injection first.
