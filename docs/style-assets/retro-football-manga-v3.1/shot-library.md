# Retro Football Manga v3.1 Shot Library

## Hook Shots

- Extreme close-up on determined eyes, reflection of a football in the pupil.
- Spinning ball fills the frame, then becomes a manga cover title.
- Retro scoreboard flickers with a question mark instead of a score.
- Two captains in tunnel silhouette, split by a vertical comic gutter.

## Football Action Shots

- Grass-level boot touch with ink speed lines.
- Ball spin close-up with paper-grain halftone.
- Shoulder-to-shoulder sprint framed by concentration lines.
- Slide tackle freeze-frame with dust and black ink burst.
- Goalkeeper save stretched across three manga panels.
- Cross-field pass drawn as a white arc over a darkened pitch.

## Tactical Shots

- Notebook page flips into overhead pitch.
- Chalk arrows become player runs.
- Pressure circles pulse once, then freeze.
- Three keyword stamps hit the page: `第一球`, `转换`, `定位球`.

## Transition Shots

- Page-turn wipe.
- Old TV scanline flash.
- Ink splash freeze-frame.
- Speed-line zoom into a character's eye.
- Scoreboard number flap without showing final result.

## Avoid

- random magic aura unrelated to football;
- unreadable dense text;
- generic poster pose with no action;
- real broadcast camera mimicry;
- realistic player faces.
