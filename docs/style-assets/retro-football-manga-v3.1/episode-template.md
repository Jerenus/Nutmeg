# Retro Football Manga v3.1 Episode Template

## Focus Match: 60 Seconds / 6 x 10s

### Segment 01: 2-Second Hook + Manga Cover

Purpose: stop the scroll and introduce the conflict.

Required visuals:

- old TV flicker or comic page opening;
- large face / ball / scoreboard suspense in the first frame;
- two fictional teams in tunnel, center circle, or stadium entrance;
- readable Chinese hook text;
- no real crest, no sponsor, no official kit.

Narration role:

- one sharp question;
- frame the match as a tactical conflict.

### Segment 02: Team A Weapon Card

Purpose: show the first team's main strength.

Required visuals:

- key fictional player close-up;
- boots, eyes, captain band, or first touch;
- one keyword card: `压迫`, `节奏`, `宽度`, `中路`, or `定位球`.

### Segment 03: Team B Counterweapon Card

Purpose: show the opponent's route to hurt the first team.

Required visuals:

- contrasting move such as counter lane, set-piece threat, or midfield escape;
- manga panel split screen;
- speed-line collision between both plans.

### Segment 04: Tactical Notebook

Purpose: convert analysis into readable visual logic.

Required visuals:

- hand-drawn notebook or chalkboard;
- arrows, zones, pressure circles;
- only 2-3 variables;
- no odds board or gambling UI.

Suggested variables:

- `第一球`
- `转换速度`
- `定位球`
- `体能`
- `边路空间`

### Segment 05: Simulated Replay

Purpose: create a memorable football-action climax.

Required visuals:

- one simulated chance, tackle, save, shot, or transition race;
- label or framing that makes it clear this is a pre-match simulation;
- dramatic eye cut-in, ball spin, ink burst, and freeze-frame impact.

### Segment 06: Retro Scoreboard + Closing Suspense

Purpose: close with a useful but compliant takeaway.

Required visuals:

- retro scoreboard or chapter-end card;
- no final score claim;
- one viewing question;
- responsible-viewing subtitle.

Required disclaimer:

`以上只是赛前数据观察，不构成任何投注建议，理性看球。`

## Standard Match: 60 Seconds / 4 x 15s

1. Hook Cover: first-frame suspense + two fictional teams.
2. Weapon Clash: Team A weapon vs Team B counterweapon.
3. Tactical Replay: notebook variables + one simulated chance.
4. Scoreboard Close: viewing question + disclaimer.
