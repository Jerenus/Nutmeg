## 5. Tactical Matchup

- **Germany strengths**: elite creative density (Musiala + Wirtz + Sané + Havertz), fullback overloads (Kimmich/Brown combined for 3 goals + an assist MD1), volume shooters (26 shots vs Curaçao). They will dominate possession and territory.
- **Germany weakness**: **chronic World Cup defensive leak — 0 clean sheets in last 7 WC games.** Schlotterbeck/Tah CB pairing exposed in transition; Germany push fullbacks very high.
- **Ivory Coast strengths**: physical, fast, dangerous on the counter — exactly the profile that has punished this Germany defence. Diomande's directness + Singo/Doué athleticism + Amad's late-game composure. AFCON champions with proven big-moment resilience (beat France, killed Ecuador's unbeaten run with a 90th-min winner).
- **Ivory Coast plan**: deep low block (as vs Ecuador), absorb, spring counters into Germany's vacated flanks.
- **Expected game state**: Germany 60–65% possession, sustained pressure; Ivory Coast compact and waiting to counter. The Germany defensive frailty + a disciplined, pacy CIV side is the core reason this is NOT a clean blowout setup.
